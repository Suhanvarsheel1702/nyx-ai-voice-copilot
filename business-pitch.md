# Nyx business pitch

## Problem

Inside-sales teams must explain a new pay-in-3 affordability product accurately on every call, navigate onboarding/KYC, and follow up after customers drop off. Manual support makes quality, compliance, and timely CRM updates inconsistent.

## Solution

Nyx is a real-time voice co-pilot. It captures consent, recognizes why the customer is calling, retrieves approved product/KYC guidance, suggests a safe next action, and drafts CRM activity and follow-up for agent approval.

## Value

- Higher conversion through consistent, timely explanations and follow-up.
- Faster agent ramp-up through in-call, source-grounded coaching.
- Lower compliance risk: consent, human oversight, and no-stale-terms guardrails are built into the flow.
- Better CRM hygiene without asking agents to write notes after every call.

## Rough unit cost

Routine intent classification is routed to a low-cost model or rule/classifier layer (demo estimate: ₹0.08 per decision). Higher-cost retrieval/reasoning is used only for ambiguous, high-value, or sensitive cases. Actual cost depends on call length, transcription vendor, model choice, and CRM/telephony contracts.

## Next

Connect approved knowledge sources, telephony/transcription, and CRM systems; establish evaluation datasets from historical calls; pilot with human QA; then measure conversion, handling time, follow-up completion, and compliance exceptions.
